STEPAHEADROBOTICS – ROBOTICS PROJECTS EDITING

1. Open robotics-projects.html in Notepad or another text editor.
2. Find the PROJECTS array near the bottom of the file.
3. Replace the sample project names and descriptions with your own.
4. Add or remove project objects as needed. The visible project numbers and enquiry dropdown update automatically.
5. The price is fixed at ₹99 per project in the current page code.
6. Visitors enter their name and WhatsApp number, choose a project number and send the pre-filled enquiry through WhatsApp. They must press Send in WhatsApp.
7. You verify payment in your payment account and send the matching project text file manually.
8. Do not add private Drive URLs to this public HTML file. Keep project files private and send them only after verifying payment.
9. WhatsApp destination is set by WHATSAPP_NUMBER in the script.
