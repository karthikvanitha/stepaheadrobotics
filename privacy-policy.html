Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This page explains how stepAheadRobotics collects and uses information submitted through this website and its enquiry forms.

For parents and guardians: Our courses and project resources are intended for young learners. A parent or legal guardian should complete or supervise forms submitted for a child.

1. Information we may collect

  • Student name and age.
  • Parent or guardian name, WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Course, level, country or time zone, and any message entered in the form.
  • For robotics project requests: name, contact number, selected project number and optional message.

2. How information is used

We use submitted details to respond to enquiries, discuss classes, process project requests, confirm payments, deliver purchased project files and provide customer support.

3. Where information is sent

If Google Sheets has been configured, using Submit Enquiry sends the form details to the connected Google Sheet. Choosing a WhatsApp enquiry opens WhatsApp with a prepared message; the visitor must press Send in WhatsApp for the message to be sent. WhatsApp and Google are third-party services and their own privacy terms also apply.

4. Sharing and protection

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be handled by the service providers needed to operate the website, forms, messaging and file delivery. Access to enquiry records should be limited to the people responsible for managing the service.

5. Retention and requests

We keep enquiry and order information only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, record-keeping, support and applicable legal obligations. A parent or guardian may request access, correction or deletion by emailing vanitha.vairakkannu@gmail.com, subject to records we are legally required to retain.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page.
