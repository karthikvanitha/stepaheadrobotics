Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy applies to paid robotics project text files requested through the stepAheadRobotics website.

1. Price and order confirmation

The listed price is ₹99 per project. Before paying, confirm the selected project number, project name and total amount with us through WhatsApp.

2. Before delivery

If you change your mind or select the wrong project, contact us as soon as possible and before the file is sent. If payment has been received and the order is cancelled before delivery, we will review the request and arrange an appropriate refund where applicable.

3. After delivery

If the file is missing, corrupted, does not match the selected project, or cannot be opened, contact us so we can correct the delivery or review a refund request. Requests after a digital file has been delivered will be assessed individually and in accordance with applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

4. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you believe you paid twice or paid an incorrect amount, send us the transaction date, amount and transaction reference through WhatsApp or email. Do not share your UPI PIN, OTP, password or other confidential payment credentials.

5. How to request help

Email vanitha.vairakkannu@gmail.com or contact us through the WhatsApp link on this website. Include your name, selected project number and payment reference. We will review the request and respond.
